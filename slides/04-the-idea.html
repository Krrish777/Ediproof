Diplomas, hashed. Held for good. 

keccak256(abi.encodePacked(
    studentName,
    courseName,
    institution,
    ipfsURI
)) → bytes32 certHash  // the on-chain anchor

Four fields go in. A 32-byte fingerprint comes out. Change one letter — even one comma — and the fingerprint changes. There is no almost match — the certificate is either authentic or it is not.

Algorithm keccak256 · SHA-3 family Output bytes32 · 256-bit digest Sensitivity 1 byte → entirely different hash
