A Diagnosis · In Four Numbers and Three Failures

Paper degrees were never a security primitive.

“Three to fourteen percent of credential dossiers contain at least one materially-altered document. Every solution paper has offered — watermarks, embossed seals, holographic foil — has been defeated by cheaper printers.”

Forgery rate 8–14% across audited credential dossiers
Per credential check $25–$80 international background-check vendors
Verification turnaround Days registrar's office, business hours only
Cryptographic guarantees 0 PDFs offer none against tampering
I.
Manual telephone verification.

Slow, expensive, business-hours only — and entirely dependent on the goodwill and staffing of the registrar's office.

II.
Centralised digi-lockers.

Limited institutional coverage, occasional portal-level downtime, and a bilateral signup requirement before any verification can occur.

III.
Generic NFT credentials.

Transferable by default — which makes them incoherent for degrees. A degree that can be sold to another wallet is not a degree.

From the Editors

“The forgery of a diploma has always been a problem of paper. Édiproof makes paper obsolete.”
