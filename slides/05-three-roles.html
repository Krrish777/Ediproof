A Three-Act Rite · & an Observer

How a certificate is impressed.

A three-act rite: an institution attests, the chain witnesses, a student holds. A fourth observer — the verifier — consults what the chain has recorded. Each act is recorded in ink that cannot be rewritten.

Act the First

The Institution attests.

A pre-approved institution opens the ledger, fills the four fields, and uploads the PDF. A single signature in MetaMask impresses the certificate upon the chain.

issueCertificate(student, name, course, institution, ipfs) EdiproofCertificate.sol:85–114
Act the Second

The Chain witnesses.

A keccak256 hash is cast from the four fields and stored beside the token id. Tampering with any byte produces a different hash — the forgery becomes instantly legible.

keccak256(abi.encodePacked(…)) EdiproofCertificate.sol:399–406, 410–420
Act the Third

The Student holds.

The certificate is minted as a soulbound token to the student's wallet. It cannot be sold, traded, or transferred. It is theirs alone, for as long as the chain endures.

_update(…) reverts on transfer EdiproofCertificate.sol:410–420
An Observer

The Verifier consults.

Anyone, with no wallet, may submit the four fields and receive the verdict. This is the only role with no MetaMask requirement — and the most important role of all.
