The Soulbound Override

Five lines of code. The whole ‘soulbound’ guarantee.

OpenZeppelin’s ERC-721 funnels every transfer through a single hook called _update. Override that hook with one condition — and the token becomes non-transferable, forever, against every present and future transfer surface.

    address to,
    uint256 tokenId,

◆    address from = _ownerOf(tokenId);

    }
    return super._update(to, tokenId, auth);
}
From the Editors The minimalism is the contribution. A soulbound implementation small enough to be audited at a glance.
I.
What it allows.

Mint (from == 0). Burn (to == 0). Both pass through unchallenged. Every certificate must enter the world, and a few must leave it — for revocation and reissuance. Those two doors stay open.

II.
What it forbids.

Every other transfer. safeTransferFrom, transferFrom, future ERC-721 extensions — all blocked at the same chokepoint, with the custom error SoulboundTransferBlocked().
